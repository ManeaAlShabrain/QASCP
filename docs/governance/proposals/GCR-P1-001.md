## Proposed GOV-004 replacement, baseline 1.1 candidate (not applied)

The product owner approves scope and a separately recorded phase exit for a named
readiness class at an immutable revision. The implementer provides reproducible
evidence. For Internal Development Readiness only, the owner may use the separate
AI-assisted assurance process defined above and accepts only internal project
risks within recorded authority; synthetic-only and no-deployment restrictions
remain binding. Every AI reviewer declares functional separation and shared-service
limitations; it never represents an independent human. Before enterprise, client
or production acceptance, actual qualified independent human security/architecture
review and applicable substantive human bilingual/regulatory/rights dispositions
are mandatory. The regulatory owner verifies applicability; the authorized operator
deploys the accepted artifact and records the outcome. Record actual people,
review processes, exact revision, readiness class and decisions. Author self-review
does not fulfill required separate review. Missing required reviews/authority or
unresolved mandatory blockers prevent the affected decision.

ENG-001 would receive a narrow cross-reference: human reviewer assessment remains
required for enterprise/client/production acceptance; internal-only assessment
must meet GOV-004's separate process. No blanket deletion of “human review” is
proposed elsewhere and AI-001 production human approval is unchanged.
